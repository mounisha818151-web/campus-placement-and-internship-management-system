ABOUT NEXHIRE

Connecting talent with opportunity.

NexHire is a campus placement and internship management platform designed to connect students with companies and career opportunities in one place.

WHAT IS NEXHIRE?

Your bridge from campus to career.

Finding the right placement or internship can be difficult when opportunities, applications and company information are spread across different platforms.

NexHire brings everything together in one centralized platform where students can discover opportunities, apply for positions and track their applications.

One Platform.

Opportunities, applications and career information — all in one place.
